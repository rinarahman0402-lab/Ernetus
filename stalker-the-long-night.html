STALKER

THE LONG NIGHT
11:47 PM.

You were walking home when the lights across the neighborhood suddenly went out.

Your phone has no signal. The streets are completely empty. Even the houses are silent.

Then you notice something strange. A figure is standing at the end of the street.

You look away for a second. When you look back... it is closer.

You have one goal tonight: find a way out before the Stalker catches you.

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

📱 On a phone:
• Use the circle on the bottom-left to move.
• Drag on the right side of the screen to look around.
• Explore the dark house and street.
• Keep watching your surroundings.

💻 On a computer:
• W / S = move forward / backward
• A / D = move left / right
• Arrow keys = look around
